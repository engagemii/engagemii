# Engagemii

We measure how visible a website is to AI answer engines.

When someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation, some
companies get named and most never do. Engagemii scores any website 0 to 10 on how
well those engines can find, read and name it, and shows what to change.

## The free check

Enter a domain at [engagemii.com](https://engagemii.com). You get a score out of 10 and
your own AI visibility page. No signup, no card.

## What we offer

Engagemii tracks more than 82 million companies and how visible each one is to AI answer
engines. Checking and fixing your own company is free: the score, your AI visibility page,
the fix kit and AI crawler monitoring. Plans are shown inside the account.

The data: 113 columns per company (105 included, 8 in optional add-ons). Records cost
10 cents each, 4 cents from 5,000; the add-ons cost 1 cent a record each, 0.4 cents from
5,000. Get them as files (CSV, JSON or Excel), through the records API or MCP server, or on
Databricks Marketplace. We never sell emails, people's names or personal phone numbers.
